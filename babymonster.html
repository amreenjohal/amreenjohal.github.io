BABYMONSTER

About BABYMONSTER

BABYMONSTER is a seven-member South Korean girl group under YG Entertainment. They are known for their powerful vocals, rap, dancing and energetic performances.

Members

  • RUKA
  • PHARITA
  • ASA
  • AHYEON
  • RAMI
  • RORA
  • CHIQUITA

Debut

BABYMONSTER officially debuted as a seven-member group on April 1, 2024. Their first mini album was called "BABYMONS7ER."

Popular Songs

  1. SHEESH
  2. BATTER UP
  3. LIKE THAT
  4. DRIP
  5. FOREVER

Fun Facts

BABYMONSTER has seven members from different countries.

Their fans are called MONSTIEZ.
