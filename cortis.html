CORTIS

About CORTIS

CORTIS is a five-member boy group from South Korea under BIGHIT MUSIC. Their name comes from the phrase "COLOR OUTSIDE THE LINES."

Members

  • MARTIN
  • JAMES
  • JUHOON
  • SEONGHYEON
  • KEONHO

Debut

CORTIS debuted on August 18, 2025. Their debut song was called "What You Want."

Popular Songs

  1. What You Want
  2. GO!
  3. FaSHioN
  4. JoyRide
  5. Lullaby

Fun Facts

All five members help create their music, choreography and videos.

Their first EP was called "COLOR OUTSIDE THE LINES."
